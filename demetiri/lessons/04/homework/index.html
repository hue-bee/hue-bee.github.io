Changing Wholes & Rates

6 items · 14 min

For 7–10, label the base of each percent. For 11–12, keep units beside every factor and cancel them before computing.

Structure before arithmeticOriginal → current → finalUnits must match
Problem 7L2

A $75 jacket is discounted 12%. Then 8% sales tax is applied to the discounted price. What is the total?

Label the discounted price before applying tax.

Problem 8L3

A tank contains 240 liters of water. The amount increases by 15%, then decreases by 20%. Find the final amount and the net percent change.

Use the new amount for the second change.

Problem 9L3

After a 30% discount, a set of books costs $49. What was the original price?

Write the fraction of the original price that remains.

Problem 10L4

A price is reduced by 25%. What percent increase from the reduced price restores the original?

Choose 100 as the original price. Give an exact fraction or mixed number.

Problem 11L2

7 folders cost $12.60. At the same unit price, what will 11 folders cost?

Use a unit rate or two correctly aligned ratios.

Problem 12L3

Each day, 5 groups each need a 3/4-hour workshop. One tutor teaches all groups in sequence for 3 days. How many teaching hours are needed?

Write hours/group × groups/day × days.
