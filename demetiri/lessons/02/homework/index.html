Part B — Ability: Paper Folding

6 Problems · ~12 min
For each item: count layers, undo the newest fold first, and reflect every existing hole the same perpendicular distance across the crease.
1 · CountPredict the final number of holes before viewing choices.
2 · ReverseOpen the most recent fold first.
3 · MirrorPreserve equal perpendicular distance from each crease.
15.One horizontal half-fold is made. The folded sheet receives one punch away from the crease. Which description must match the unfolded sheet? L2
16.A square is folded left to right and then bottom to top. One punch is made away from both creases. What is guaranteed after both unfolds? L2
17.The paper is folded on the shown diagonal. Which statement locates the partner hole after unfolding? L3
18.A square is folded in half vertically. Two punches are made in the folded sheet, both away from the crease and at different heights. How many holes should appear after unfolding? L2
19.After one vertical half-fold, a punch is made exactly on the folded crease. What changes about the usual doubling rule? L3
20.A sheet is folded vertically first and diagonally second. Which crease is reversed first, and what happens to the existing punch? L4
