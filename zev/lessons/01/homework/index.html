Part A — Expressions & Translation

8 Questions · ~14 min

Before choosing, identify the danger step: distribution, sign, like-term match, grouping, or word order. Then commit.

1. Which expression is equivalent to 7a − 4 + 2a + 11? L1

Point to the two like-term pairs.

2. Simplify −4(3x − 2) + 5. L2

Show both deliveries of −4.

3. Which expression is equivalent to 6 − 3(2y + 5)? L2

Protect the sign before distributing.

4. Simplify ½(10p − 8) − 3p. L3

Distribute before combining.

5. In 4m²n − 3mn² + 7m²n − 5, which two terms are like terms? L3

Match variable letters and exponents.

6. Which expression means “four less than three times the sum of n and 2”? L3

Box the quantity multiplied by 3.

7. Which expression represents “one third of the difference between 15 and q”? L3

Build the difference first.

8. A rectangle has width w and length w + 5. Which expression gives its perimeter? L4

Write P = 2L + 2W first.

Part B — Multi-Step Equations

8 Questions · ~18 min

Write one legal equation line before selecting an answer. Substitute your result into the original before committing.

1 · Distribute2 · Combine3 · Collect4 · Isolate5 · Verify

9. Solve 6x − 11 = 31. L1

State the inverse operation.

10. Solve 9 − 2n = 23. L2

The coefficient is −2, not 2.

11. Solve 5x + 18 = 2x − 6. L2

Keep the remaining coefficient positive if possible.

12. Solve 4(2y + 3) = 3y − 8. L3

Clear parentheses before moving terms.

13. Solve (q − 5)/3 = 7. L2

Undo the outer operation first.

14. Solve 0.4m + 6 = 18. L3

Estimate before dividing by 0.4.

15. How many solutions does 3(2t − 1) = 6t + 4 have? L4

Interpret the statement left after variables cancel.

16. A bicycle rental costs $12 plus $4.50 per hour. The total charge is $43.50. How many hours was the bicycle rented? L3

Write the fee equation before computing.
