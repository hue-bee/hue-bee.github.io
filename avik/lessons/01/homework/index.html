7. Evaluate: −8 − (−13). L1

Answer: C. Subtracting a negative gives −8 + 13 = 5.
Choose, audit, then commit.

8. Which fraction is equivalent to 0.375? L2

Answer: B. 0.375 = 375/1000 = 3/8.
Choose, audit, then commit.

9. A school club scheduled 29 events. Five were canceled. Of the events held, there were three times as many indoor events as outdoor events. How many outdoor events were held? L3

events
Answer: 6. After 5 cancellations, 24 events remain. One outdoor part plus three indoor parts gives 4 equal parts, so 24 ÷ 4 = 6.
Choose, audit, then commit.

10. A rental shop has 31 scooters and tricycles. Together they have 81 wheels. How many tricycles are there? L3

Answer: C. A 2-wheel baseline gives 62 wheels; the 19 extra wheels identify 19 tricycles.
Choose, audit, then commit.

11. Two storage shelves hold 164 folders. The upper shelf holds 26 more folders than the lower shelf. How many folders are on the lower shelf? L3

Answer: B. Remove the extra 26, then split: (164 − 26) ÷ 2 = 69.
Choose, audit, then commit.

12. Seven identical notebooks cost $24.50. At the same rate, what is the cost of 12 notebooks? L2

dollars
Answer: $42. Each notebook costs $24.50 ÷ 7 = $3.50; 12 × $3.50 = $42.
Choose, audit, then commit.

13. Select both expressions equal to 35% of 240. L2

Select exactly 2 answers.

Answer: A and C. Both 0.35 and 35/100 represent 35%.
Choose, audit, then commit.

14. A rectangle’s length is 3 inches greater than its width. Its perimeter is 38 inches. What is the rectangle’s area? L4

Answer: B. If width is w, then 2w + 2(w + 3) = 38, so w = 8 and length = 11; area = 88 square inches.
Choose, audit, then commit.

Part C — Standalone ELA

15. Which revision corrects the misplaced modifier?
After reviewing the blueprints, the doorway measurement seemed incorrect to Leila. L3

Answer: C. Leila—not the measurement—reviewed the blueprints.
Choose, audit, then commit.

16. Choose the punctuation that best completes the sentence:
The museum closed at six ___ however, the restoration crew remained until midnight. L2

Answer: B. A semicolon may join the independent clauses before the conjunctive adverb “however.”
Choose, audit, then commit.

17. Which revision combines the sentences most effectively?
The library installed motion sensors. The sensors reduce electricity use in empty rooms. L2

Answer: C. The relative clause clearly and concisely explains what the sensors do.
Choose, audit, then commit.

18. The first prototype was inexpensive to build. ___, it failed when exposed to heavy rain. Which transition best completes the text? L2

Answer: B. “However” signals the contrast between low cost and poor performance.

Part D — ELA Passage Set

4 Questions · ~12 min

Read for problem → response → result. You may revise any answer in this set until selecting “Submit passage set.”

ELA passage set · editable until submission

A Garden That Collects Rain

[1] A Queens science class noticed that puddles lingered beside its school long after storms ended. [2] Rather than request a costly replacement of the pavement, students mapped where water collected and measured how long the area took to drain.

[3] They designed a shallow garden that was filled with native grasses and porous soil. [4] During installation, volunteers lowered the garden below sidewalk level so runoff could flow into it.

[5] During the first heavy storm after installation, the garden captured runoff; by the next morning, the former puddle area was nearly dry. [6] The students plan to compare drainage records each season and replace plants that fail to thrive.

19. Which statement best expresses the central idea of the passage? L3

Answer: B. The passage follows the problem, student design, installation, and result.

20. Why was the garden installed below sidewalk level? L2

Answer: C. Sentence 4 states that the lower level lets runoff flow into the garden.

21. Which sentence provides the strongest evidence that the design improved drainage? L3

Answer: D. Sentence 5 reports that the former puddle area was nearly dry the next morning.

22. Which revision of sentence 3 is most concise while preserving its meaning? L4

Answer: D. The revision removes unnecessary words without losing information.
Answer all four; review evidence before submitting.
