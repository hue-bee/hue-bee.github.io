Passage Set 1 — The Last Ferry

For three summers, Tessa had recorded the harbor's morning sounds for a community history project. Most days, her files began with gulls, then ropes knocking softly against masts, and finally the ferry horn at 6:40. On the first Monday in September, the horn never sounded. Tessa checked her watch twice before noticing a printed notice taped inside the empty ticket booth.

Instead of packing away her recorder, she walked to the end of the pier. A mechanic was testing the engine of a small electric launch that would begin carrying passengers the following week. Tessa captured its low hum, the slap of water against its narrow hull, and the mechanic calling measurements to a coworker. That evening, she renamed the day's file “Harbor, Between Routes.”
