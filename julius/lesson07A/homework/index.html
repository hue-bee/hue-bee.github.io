Part C — Guided Level 5 Stretch

3 Challenges ·

These are integrated problems, not a speed test. Use the three planning boxes, then write and commit one final response.

Challenge 13Level 5Open

A small rectangular display box measures 4 cm by 6 cm by 10 cm. A larger similar box has 8 times the volume. The larger box is painted on the outside except for its bottom. One milliliter of paint covers 28 cm². How many milliliters of paint are needed?

1 · SCALERecover the edge multiplier from the volume multiplier.
2 · INVENTORYCount the lid and four walls, but not the bottom.
3 · CONVERTDivide painted area by the coverage rate.
Keep length, area, volume, and paint units separate.
Challenge 14Level 5Open

Of 150 students, 82 join drama, 65 join music, and 21 join neither. Two different students are selected at random from those in at least one club. What is the probability that one is drama-only and the other is music-only, in either order?

1 · ELIGIBLERemove students in neither club from the sample space.
2 · REGIONSFind the overlap, then both “only” groups.
3 · PATHSCount both orders without replacement.
“At least one” defines who is eligible; “only” defines success.
Challenge 15Level 5Open

Tank A holds 80 L of red and blue liquid in a 3:1 ratio. Tank B holds 40 L in a 1:3 ratio. Some well-mixed liquid is transferred from A to B. Tank B’s new red-to-blue ratio is 1:1. What percentage of Tank A’s original liquid was transferred?

1 · COMPOSITIONFind the red fraction of the liquid that moves.
2 · TARGETExpress Tank B’s final red amount as a fraction of its new total.
3 · BASEConvert transferred liters using Tank A’s original 80 L.
Liters transferred are not yet the requested percentage.
