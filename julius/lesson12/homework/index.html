Part B — ELA: Revising & Editing (Passage Set)

12 Questions · ~20 min

Read the draft passage once for overall meaning. For each numbered spot, decide whether a revision improves clarity, fixes a transition, or removes an off-topic sentence. Choose the best replacement.

Draft: Why Sleep Matters for Teenagers

[1] Many teenagers do not get enough sleep during the school year. [2] Experts recommend that teens sleep between eight and ten hours each night, but surveys show that most teens sleep closer to six or seven hours. [3] This gap between recommended and actual sleep has real consequences for how well students learn and perform.

[4] One major reason for the shortfall is early school start times. [5] Many high schools begin classes before 8:00 a.m., which forces teenagers to wake up while their bodies are still producing melatonin, a hormone that promotes sleepiness. [6] My favorite subject in school is actually science class. [7] As a result, students arrive at school groggy and have trouble concentrating during morning lessons.

[8] Sleep deprivation does more than make students tired, it can also affect memory, mood, and decision-making. [9] Studies have linked chronic sleep loss in teenagers to lower test scores and a higher risk of anxiety. [10] Some schools have responded by delaying their start times, and early results suggest that students at these schools show improved attendance and grades.

[11] Parents and students can also take steps on their own to improve sleep habits. [12] Turning off screens an hour before bed, keeping a consistent bedtime, and avoiding caffeine in the evening can all help teens fall asleep faster. [13] With small changes at home and bigger changes at school, teenagers can get the rest their growing bodies and busy minds truly need.

Q1. Which transition word BEST replaces "As a result" in sentence [7] to keep the logical flow after the off-topic sentence [6] is removed? L2
Q2. Which sentence should be DELETED because it is off-topic? L2
Q3. Which is the BEST revised topic sentence for the second paragraph (replacing [4])? L3
Q4. Sentence [8] contains a punctuation error. Which correction fixes it? L3
Q5. Which sentence would BEST be added between [9] and [10] to strengthen the transition? L3
Q6. Which revision of sentence [11] uses a more precise transition to connect to the previous paragraph's idea about school-level changes? L4
Q7. Which best restates sentence [3] using a stronger, more academic tone? L2
Q8. Which word in sentence [12] should be changed for parallel structure with the list of habits? L3
Q9. Which sentence, if inserted after [2], BEST supports the passage's main idea with a specific detail? L3
Q10. Which is the clearest, most concise revision of sentence [13] to serve as a strong concluding sentence? L2
Q11. If the writer wanted to add a sentence citing a specific study's findings for extra support, where would it fit BEST? L4
Q12. Which best describes the overall organization of this passage? L4
