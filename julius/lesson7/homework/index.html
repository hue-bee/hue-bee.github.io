Part E: Stated Detail or Inference?

3 Problems

A stated detail is written directly in the passage. An inference is a reasonable conclusion built from evidence the passage gives you, without saying it outright.

Sentence stem"The text says ___, which means ___."
Passage
Mara refilled her water bottle three times during the two-hour hike and stopped twice in the shade before the group reached the summit.
Problem 12 L2

Is the statement "Mara felt tired and overheated during the hike" a stated detail or an inference?

Problem 13 L3

What can you reasonably infer about the difficulty of the hike, and which exact phrase from the passage is your evidence?

Passage
The company rewrote its return policy for the third time this year and posted a notice apologizing to customers for "recent inconvenience."
Problem 14 L4

Which is the stronger inference about the company: it is responsive to customer feedback, or it has struggled to get its return policy right the first time? Defend your choice using specific details from the passage — not just a guess.
