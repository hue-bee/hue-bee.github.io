Part F — ELA: Stated Detail or Inference?

4 Questions · ~12 min

An inference needs one logical step beyond what's written. Use the frame: "The text says ___, which means ___."

Q16. A passage states: "By 1920, the factory employed three times as many workers as it had in 1900." Is the statement "the factory grew" a stated detail or an inference? L2
Q17. A character "counted her coins twice before buying the bread." What can you reasonably infer about her financial situation, and what one phrase is your evidence? L3
Q18. A passage says: "He wore his winter coat, even though it was only September." What can you infer about the weather or the character's habits? L1
Q19. A scientist "double-checked her data three times and asked a colleague to review it before publishing." Which is the stronger inference: she is meticulous, or she is uncertain about her results? Defend your choice using the specific details given. L4
SHSAT Trap: A correct inference is one small logical step from the text — not a wild guess. If an answer choice requires outside knowledge the passage never hints at, it's a trap.
