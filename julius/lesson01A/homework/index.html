"The Spare Key" — Fiction Excerpt

[¶1] Deja pressed the spare key into her palm until the metal left a faint red line across her skin. Her mother had given it to her that morning without ceremony — just a quick "hold onto this" before rushing out the door for a shift that wouldn't end until midnight. Deja had wanted to ask why tonight, of all nights, but the question stuck somewhere behind her teeth.

[¶2] Her older brother Marcus was supposed to be home by six. Instead, the porch light clicked on by itself at dusk, and the driveway stayed empty. Deja sat on the front step anyway, key still in her fist, watching every car that slowed near the corner and then kept going.

[¶3] By eight o'clock, she had stopped watching the street and started watching the clock on her phone instead — 8:04, 8:11, 8:19 — each minute heavier than the last. When headlights finally swung into the driveway at 8:26, she didn't run to the door. She sat very still, the key digging into her palm again, and waited to see who would get out first.

[¶4] It was Marcus, alone, walking slower than usual, one hand pressed against his ribs. "Don't tell Mom," he said before Deja could ask anything. Even so, his voice cracked on the word "Mom," and Deja understood, in that instant, that whatever had happened had scared him more than he wanted to admit.

[¶5] Deja unclenched her fist and held out the spare key. "You should hold this," she said. "You're the one who keeps forgetting yours." It wasn't what she meant to say — not really — but it was the only way she knew to say I'm glad you're okay without saying it directly.

Q1. What does Deja want in this excerpt? L1
Q2. What blocks Deja from getting what she wants for most of the passage? L1
Q3. Where does the tone shift most clearly, and toward what feeling? L2
Q4. What does Marcus's cracked voice on the word "Mom" (¶4) reveal that the narration doesn't state directly? L2
Q5. In ¶4, the phrase "Even so" signals a relationship of: L2
Q6. What does Deja's final line, "You should hold this... you're the one who keeps forgetting yours" (¶5), really mean? L3
Q7. Which statement best describes how the passage is organized across all five paragraphs? L3
Q8. A student claims: "Deja gives Marcus the key because she no longer wants that responsibility." Using evidence from ¶1 and ¶5, is this claim supported, partially supported, or contradicted? L4
