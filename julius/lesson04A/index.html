Ratio Core · Setup Protocol

Complex Ratios Fail When Labels Disappear

Ask before calculating
What does each number count? What unit is attached? Is it a part, another part, or the whole? Which two quantities does the question actually compare?
Write the setup in words first
quantity + unit + role → ratio → scale
Required spoken line: “This number counts ___ in ___ units, and it is the ___ part.”
Ratio Anatomy

Part-to-Part Is Not Part-to-Whole

Look at the same diagram three ways
If the strip has 2 dark blocks, 3 medium blocks, and 5 light blocks, which comparison uses 10 as a term? Which comparisons never use 10?
Part : partCompare two categories only.
Part : wholeAdd every category before comparing.
Whole : partOrder matters; reverse only if asked.
Guided Deconstruction 1

Three-Term Ratio With A Changed Part

Model situation: Red : blue : green marbles = 2 : 3 : 5, with 80 marbles total. Blue marbles are added until red : blue becomes 1 : 2. How many blue marbles were added?
Step 1 · Build the whole
Total ratio parts: 2 + 3 + 5 = 10. Each ratio part represents 80 ÷ 10 = 8 marbles.
Step 2 · Name the unchanged anchor
Red stays fixed: 2 × 8 = 16. Initial blue: 3 × 8 = 24.
Step 3 · Rebuild only the changed part
For red : blue = 1 : 2, new blue is 2 × 16. Amount added: (2 × 16) − 24 = finish
The pathway is shown; the final arithmetic is deliberately left for the student.

Embedded Unit Changes

A Ratio Cannot Compare Different Rulers

Why is 3 cups : 2 quarts not yet ready?
The numbers are measured with different-sized units. Convert first so both sides speak the same measurement language; then simplify or scale.
same kind + same unit → valid ratio
SHSAT trap: converting the wrong direction
Choose the unit that makes the next step easiest. Do not convert automatically; convert strategically.
Guided Deconstruction 2

Units First, Scale Second

Model situation: A drink uses 3 cups of concentrate for every 2 quarts of water. If a batch uses 14 quarts of water, how many cups of concentrate are needed?
Step 1 · Match units
2 quarts = 8 cups, so the recipe ratio is 3 cups : 8 cups.
Step 2 · Convert the target
14 quarts = 56 cups of water. Now both the recipe and target use cups.
Step 3 · Scale one role
Concentrate needed: (3/8) × 56 = finish. Keep “cups concentrate” attached.
Never mix the conversion factor with the recipe factor in one unlabeled line.

Scaling Recipes & Mixtures

Scale the Batch, Not One Ingredient Alone

What stays invariant?
The ingredient ratio stays fixed. The amount of each ingredient changes by the same scale factor, so the flavor or concentration remains unchanged.
Find partsAdd only when the whole mixture matters.
Find scaleCompare known amount to its matching role.
Apply scaleMultiply every ingredient by the same factor.

Combining Ratios Across Groups

Never Add Ratios Until They Become Counts

Can you add 2 : 3 and 3 : 5 directly?
Not safely. Each ratio may use a different “part size.” Turn each group's ratio into actual counts, combine matching categories, then form the final ratio.
ratio → counts → combine roles → new ratio
False shortcut
Adding corresponding terms gives 5 : 8 only when the hidden scale factors happen to match. The group totals decide whether they match.
Guided Deconstruction 3

Two Groups, Two Scale Factors

Model situation: Choir A has a boys : girls ratio of 2 : 3 and 25 students. Choir B has a boys : girls ratio of 3 : 5 and 40 students. Find the combined boys : girls ratio.
Step 1 · Decode A
A has 5 ratio parts, so each part is 25 ÷ 5 = 5. Counts: boys 2 × 5; girls 3 × 5.
Step 2 · Decode B
B has 8 ratio parts, so each part is 40 ÷ 8 = 5. Counts: boys 3 × 5; girls 5 × 5.
Step 3 · Combine matching roles
New ratio: (2×5 + 3×5) : (3×5 + 5×5). Simplify to finish.
Ratios describe structure; counts are what can be added.

Accuracy Protocol

The 15-Second Ratio Audit

Check 1Labels
Can every number answer “how many what?”
Restore any dropped units or roles before trusting the setup.
Check 2Order
Does the ratio follow the question's noun order?
Read the numerator and denominator as words, not just symbols.
Check 3Scale
Did all unchanged roles scale together?
Estimate the size and verify the final unit matches the prompt.
