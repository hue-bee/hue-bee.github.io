Part A — Ratios & Percent Structure

8 Questions · ~16 min

Build the multiplier or unit relationship before calculating. These are new transfer problems—not repeats from the lesson deck.

Before choices:name the basewrite the unitestimate the scale
1.Successive percent changeL2

A community garden harvested 40% more tomatoes in June than in May. In July it harvested 25% fewer tomatoes than in June. Compared with May, the July harvest was—

Audit: Does the second percent use May or June as its base?

Model: start × ___ × ___
2.Reverse percentL3

A bicycle is marked down 15%. A 6% sales tax is then applied. The final price is $720.80. What was the original price, in dollars?

Audit: final ÷ 1.06 ÷ 0.85
3.Part-rate-wholeL3

Mina saved $36, which was 12% of her earnings. Theo saved $45, which was 9% of his earnings. How much more did Theo earn than Mina?

Recover each whole separately.
4.Unit rateL2

A trail mix recipe uses 718 pound of raisins and 7 cups of cereal. How many cups of cereal are used per pound of raisins?

Scale check: denominator is less than 1 pound.
5.Capacity & complete groupsL4

A 2.75-gallon container is full. After 6 cups are reserved, the rest fills jars. How many more complete jars can be filled using 12 cup per jar than using 34 cup per jar? Use 1 gallon = 16 cups.

Do not round a partial jar up.
6.Multiple-select · percentsL3

Select all expressions equal to 35% of 240.

Multiple-select: test every option.
7.Inline choice · structureL2

Complete the reasoning: “A 30% increase followed by a 30% decrease is modeled by , because each percent acts on the .”

Both blanks must fit mathematically.
8.Ratio scale factorL3

A map uses 1.5 centimeters to represent 12 kilometers. On the same map, two towns are 4 centimeters apart. What is the actual distance, in kilometers?

Audit the direction of the scale factor.

Part B — Algebra Language & Constraints

8 Questions · ~18 min

Translate named quantities into brackets first. Then preserve order, parity, positivity, and denominator restrictions.

9.Signed translationL1

What is the result when the sum of −5 and 2 is subtracted from the product of −4 and 6?

Audit the order: the sum is subtracted from the product.

Write [product] − [sum].
10.Reciprocal & inverseL2

Which expression represents the reciprocal of −58 added to the additive inverse of −3?

Translate each noun phrase separately.
11.Fraction equationL4

Solve: 34(2x3 − 12) = x5 − 98

Verify by substitution.
12.Multiple-select · constraintsL4

Given p = q + 5 = 3r = s2, where p, q, r, s are positive integers, select all values that could be p.

Test positivity and divisibility for each candidate.
13.Rational expressionL3

For b ≠ 0, 5a2b − □6b = ab. Which expression belongs in the box?

Use one common denominator.
14.Consecutive integersL3

The sum of five consecutive even integers is −130. What is the sum of the two smallest integers?

Even integers differ by 2.
15.Expression translationL2

Which expression means “the reciprocal of the sum of x and 4, decreased by 2”?

Bracket each named quantity.
16.Interactive number lineL3

Solve −3(2x − 1) > 15. Which description matches the solution?

Part C — Data, Probability & Geometry

8 Questions · ~18 min

Treat the diagram or table as evidence. Write the weighted total, scale factors, or changing counts before dividing.

17.Unequal area scalingL2

A rectangle’s length is multiplied by 3 while its width is multiplied by 12. The new area is what fraction of the original area?

Area factor = length factor × width factor.
18.Circle & inscribed tilesL3

A circle has area 81π square centimeters. Six congruent squares are arranged edge to edge across the circle’s diameter. What is the total area of the six squares?

Use the diameter before square area.

20.Weighted estimateL3

A distribution has 2 values at 10, 3 values at 20, 9 values at 30, and 6 values at 40. Without fully calculating, which interval must contain the mean?

Locate the balance point before computing.
21.Changing probabilityL4

The probability of selecting a green token from a bag is 38. After 6 green tokens are removed, the probability becomes 14. How many tokens were originally in the bag?

Model the before and after states.
22.Data claims · multiple-selectL3

A frequency table lists values 2, 5, and 9 with frequencies 4, 3, and 1, respectively. Select all true statements.

Expand mentally or use value × frequency.
23.Uniform scaleL2

Every length of a scale drawing is multiplied by 2.5. By what factor does its area change?

Area is two-dimensional.
