Part D — ELA: Revising & Editing

6 Questions · ~15 min

Read each sentence carefully. Look for redundancy, dangling modifiers, and unclear pronoun references before choosing an answer.

Revision Practice Set

Editors do not just shorten sentences — they make sure every word earns its place and every phrase points clearly back to the right subject. A sentence can be grammatically correct and still confuse a reader if a modifier is misplaced or if a pronoun could refer to more than one noun. The six questions below ask you to find the specific problem in each sentence, not just to guess which choice "sounds better."

Q1. "After finishing her homework early, the television was turned on." What is wrong with this sentence?
Q2. "The reason the test was hard is due to the fact that it covered too much material." What kind of error is this?
Q3. Which revision best fixes: "Maria told her sister that she had won the contest."
Q4. "Each and every student must return back their permission form by Friday." Identify the redundancy.
Q5. "Running quickly to catch the bus, my backpack fell off my shoulder." Choose the best revision.
Q6. A student says: "'No Change' is always a safe choice if I'm not sure which answer is right." Is this a good test-taking strategy?
