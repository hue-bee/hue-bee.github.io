Compact Source Map

Space Peppers: Build the Skeleton

1Chile peppers grow inside a controlled habitat on the International Space Station. Their leaves, stems, and fruit develop without ordinary gravity, weather, or insects.

2After a 137-day cycle, astronauts harvest the peppers, keep some for eating, and return others to Earth for study.

3–6Fresh crops matter because packaged food and stored supplements lose nutritional value over long missions.

7–8Scientists select and engineer a pepper suited to limited space, reliable growth, and astronaut nutrition.

9An expert argues future missions should keep replanting crops instead of ending after one batch.

¶1–2
What happened
Controlled growth → harvest → eat/study.
¶3–6
Why it matters
Long missions need dependable fresh nutrition.
¶7–8
How it was designed
Space constraints shaped the pepper itself.
¶9
What comes next
Scale from one batch to continuous growth.
One-sentence gist
Can you connect all four jobs using “because” and “so”?
