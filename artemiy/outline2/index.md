## 2. Math: What Must Be Fixed (Priority Order)

### 2.1 Geometry — HIGHEST PRIORITY
**Evidence**
- Kweller: Angles & Polygons 2/6 (missed #58, #82, #94, #105); Circles 0/1 (#62: answered −6, correct 1)
- ibidPREP: Volume 0/2, Geometric Principles 0/1, Area 2/3

**Mastery targets**
- Interior/exterior angle sums: (n − 2)·180; exterior angles sum to 360
- Supplementary, complementary, vertical, and parallel-line angle pairs
- Triangle inequality, isosceles/equilateral rules, angle-side relationships
- Circumference, area of circles, arc length and sector area
- Volume and surface area of rectangular prisms, cubes, cylinders
- Composite-figure area by decomposition / subtraction
- Coordinate plane: distance, midpoint, slope, reflections
- Must state every formula from memory and identify which formula a diagram is hiding

### 2.2 Percent, Rates, and Proportions

## 3. Sequenced 11-Month Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundations (Nov 2026 – Jan 2027)
**Time:** ~3 hrs/week Math, 45 min/week ELA
**Approach:** one topic at a time, untimed first, then timed.

| Weeks | Math Topic |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Angles, polygons, triangles |
| 3–4 | Circles and 3-D solids (volume, surface area) |
| 5–6 | Percent (change, reverse, successive) |
| 7–8 | Ratios, rates, unit conversions |
| 9–10 | Linear equations, inequalities, translating words to math |
| 11–12 | Number sense, sequences, probability, grid-in drills |

**Weekly rhythm**
- Mon: learn/review rules, build formula sheet entry
- Mid-week: 25 untimed topic problems
- Sat: 15-problem timed quiz
- Sun: error-log review (re-do every miss cold)

**Milestones**
- Self-written one-page formula sheet, recited weekly
- One timed full Math section at end of January (benchmark)

### Phase 2 — Mixed Practice (Feb – Apr 2027)
- Mixed sets of 20–25 problems; student must identify the topic himself
- Add 5 grid-ins per session
- Practice "solve before looking at the choices"
- Alternate weeks: one full timed Math section / one targeted-review week
- **Target by April:** 46+/57 Math

### Phase 3 — Full Tests and Timing (May – Aug 2027)
- One full-length test every two weeks, in test order, on a digital platform (practice tech-enhanced items)
- Between tests: re-do every missed problem cold, then a second problem of the same type
- Summer push: geometry and percent to 90%+ accuracy
- **Target by August:** 50+/57 Math, 53+/57 ELA

### Phase 4 — Polish (Sep 2027 – Test Day)
- Weekly full test; taper to light review in the final 10 days
- Pacing rule: no points for finishing, no guessing penalty — do fewer questions carefully, bubble something for every question
- Final review of formula sheet and error log
