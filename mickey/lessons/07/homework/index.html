Text Purpose
Early theater programs often listed only the leading performers. Payroll records, however, identify scene painters, carpenters, and costume makers whose work the programs omit. By reading these records alongside the programs, historians can reconstruct a production as a collective undertaking rather than as the achievement of its stars alone.

Which choice best describes the main purpose of the text?

R04Level 3
Cross-Text Connections

Text 1
Because rooftop temperature sensors are easy to install across a city, their readings should be sufficient for identifying the streets where pedestrians face the greatest heat exposure.

Text 2
Rooftop sensors can reveal broad temperature patterns. Yet a shaded sidewalk and an exposed sidewalk may differ greatly even when nearby roofs have similar temperatures. Evaluating pedestrian exposure therefore also requires measurements at street level.

How would the author of Text 2 most likely respond to the claim in Text 1?

Meet the Exact Rhetorical Goal

R15–R16

Use the notes selectively. A true statement is not enough: it must meet the student’s goal without making an unsupported comparison.

R15Level 2
Rhetorical Synthesis

While researching a topic, a student has taken the following notes:

  • Mara Vale and Ivo Chen are fictional textile artists.
  • Vale makes woven panels using discarded cotton clothing.
  • Chen makes knotted sculptures using discarded nylon fishing line.
  • Both artists incorporate previously used materials into new artworks.

The student wants to emphasize a similarity between the artists. Which choice most effectively uses relevant information from the notes to accomplish this goal?

R16Level 4
Rhetorical Synthesis

While researching an invented experiment, a student has taken the following notes:

  • Two coatings were tested on identical metal plates.
  • Coating S reduced corrosion by 75% relative to an uncoated plate and cost $6 per plate.
  • Coating T reduced corrosion by 90% relative to an uncoated plate and cost $11 per plate.
  • No other coating properties were measured.

The student wants to emphasize a trade-off between corrosion reduction and cost. Which choice most effectively uses relevant information from the notes to accomplish this goal?

Your Two-Pass Contract

Proof · pace · repair

Pass 1: answer normal items now

Before calculating, name the target. Before committing, produce one piece of evidence. Make a provisional choice when possible. Suggested active flag budget: 2–3 genuine obstacles, not a required number.

Pass 2: return with a new move

Restate the task and try one productive move. If about 30 seconds pass with no useful progress, move on. Spend longer when progress is real. Practice review reserves: about 2 minutes RW and 3 minutes Math.

Proof: I can point to the words, equation, or diagram that justifies my choice.

Pace: I check the clock at planned checkpoints, not after every question.

Flags: I answer normal items immediately and return only with a new move.
