R08: Make the Claim Earn Its Evidence

1

Identify the measured comparison

Same water, soil, and temperature; shade is the changed condition. Which pair of germination rates should you compare?

2

Match direction, not decoration

Write “with shade ___ without shade.” Does the proposed evidence preserve the direction shown in the bars?

3

Keep the conclusion inside the trial

Does the option describe these results, or claim a rule about every seed? Choose independently; no choice is marked here.

47–55 minutes · use only if selected

M12: Turn a Chord into a Triangle

1

Use the perpendicular-bisector fact

A perpendicular from the center to a chord bisects the chord. Label AM as 8 ÷ 2.

2

Build the hidden radius

Draw OA. In right triangle OMA, set up OA² = 3² + (8 ÷ 2)². Which side is the radius?

3

Convert radius into area

Use A = πr². Substitute your expression for r²; simplify and select independently.

47–55 minutes · use only if selected

M13: One Root Means One Contact

1

Translate the condition

“Exactly one distinct real solution” means a repeated root. What must happen to the discriminant?

2

Substitute with the sign intact

Here a = 1, b = −10, and c = k. Set (−10)² − 4(1)k = 0. Why do the parentheses matter?

3

Isolate, then verify a second way

Solve that equation for k independently. Then complete the square in x² − 10x + k to verify that the graph touches the x-axis once.

55–60 minutes · pacing card

Your Two-Pass Contract

Pass 1: answer normal items now

Before calculating, name the target. Before committing, produce one piece of evidence.

Flag only a genuine obstacle. Make a provisional selection before moving when possible. Suggested active flag budget: 2–3; do not manufacture flags.

Pass 2: return with a new move

Suggested review reserve: about 2 minutes in RW and 3 minutes in Math.

When stuck, restate the task and try one productive move. If nothing changes after roughly 30 seconds, move on; an item may deserve longer if progress is real.

These are adjustable tutoring rules, not an official routing threshold or scoring formula.
