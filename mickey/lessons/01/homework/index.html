Part B Continued — Rhetorical Synthesis

Questions 7–12 · 7 min

Read the rhetorical goal first. Use only notes that serve that goal. A choice may be true and still be off-task.

Three-step filter

  • Goal: underline the action—compare, emphasize, introduce, explain, or qualify.
  • Notes: keep only facts needed to complete that action.
  • Shape: predict what the correct sentence must contain before reading choices.
