Probe 1 · Geometry & Trigonometry

The One Rule for All Six: Relationship First

SeeStructure
Circle the asked quantity. Mark equal angles, parallel lines, right angles, radii, and units.
SayRelationship
Complete the sentence: “Because ___, ___ must equal / scale / satisfy ___.”
SolveArithmetic
Only now compute. If Desmos cannot express the relationship faster, keep the reasoning path.
Tutor prompt
What fact would still be true if every number disappeared from the diagram? That fact is usually the governing relationship.
Geometry Probe · Item 1

Parallel Lines: Mark the Angle Family

G1Level 2
Lines m and n are parallel. The marked angles measure (3x + 18)° and (5x − 22)°. What is x?
Ask before calculating
Where do the two angles sit relative to the transversal? Equal or supplementary?
Clever path: name the angle pair, write one equation, then solve. Do not angle-chase what the parallel-line rule gives immediately.
Geometry Probe · Item 2

Similar Triangles: Match Position, Not Appearance

G2Level 3
In triangle ABC, D lies on AB, E lies on AC, and DE ∥ BC. If AD=6, DB=3, AE=8, and EC=y, find y.
Ask before the proportion
Which vertex in the small triangle corresponds to each vertex in the large triangle? Are you comparing part-to-part or part-to-whole?
Clever path: write the vertex order first. A correct similarity statement makes the proportion almost automatic.
Geometry Probe · Item 3

Right-Triangle Trig: Name the Sides From the Angle

G3Level 3
A right triangle has a 34° angle. The leg adjacent to that angle is 11, and the opposite leg is h. Find h to the nearest tenth.
Two-word setup
Which two side names appear—opposite, adjacent, or hypotenuse? Which trig ratio contains exactly those two?
Clever path: SOH–CAH–TOA is not a chant; it is a filter. Keep only the ratio containing the named known and unknown sides.
Geometry Probe · Item 4

Sector Area: Take the Same Fraction of the Whole

G4Level 3
A circle has radius 9. A sector has central angle 80°. What is the area of the sector?
Ask before using a formula
What fraction of a full turn is 80°? Should that same fraction multiply circumference or area?
sector = (central angle / 360°) × whole circle
Geometry Probe · Item 5

Volume & Units: Convert Before You Multiply

G5Level 4
A rectangular storage box measures 1.5 m by 80 cm by 0.4 m. What is its volume in cubic centimeters?
Unit checkpoint
Can three lengths enter one multiplication while wearing different units? Which single unit should every edge use first?
Clever path: units are part of the numbers. Make all three edge labels match, then compute once.
Geometry Probe · Item 6

Coordinate Circle: Build Two Perfect Squares

G6Level 4
The equation of a circle is x² + y² − 8x + 6y − 11 = 0. What is the radius?
Complete, but keep balanced
What number turns x² − 8x into a perfect square? What number does the same for y² + 6y? What must happen to both sides?
(x − h)² + (y − k)² = r² · stop only after identifying which quantity is squared

Probe 2 · Expression of Ideas

Choices Stay Covered Until You Name the Task

Transitions
Read the sentence before and after the blank. State the relationship in plain language: contrast, result, example, continuation, sequence, or clarification.
Ideas → relationship → transition family
Rhetorical Synthesis
Read the goal first. Select only notes that satisfy it. Predict the needed structure before judging answer wording.
Goal → needed notes → sentence shape
High-score trap: true but off-task
A choice can be completely supported by the text and still fail the requested rhetorical job.

Transitions: Predict the Bridge

E1Level 2

E2Level 3

Expression of Ideas · Item 3

Transition Precision: Example or Clarification?

E3Level 4
The archive contains several firsthand accounts of the expedition. ______, it includes letters written by two crew members during the voyage.
Clever distinction
Example: one instance among many possible instances.
Specification: a broad statement becomes more exact.
Clarification: the same idea is restated more plainly.
Test the sentence
Does sentence two merely illustrate “accounts,” or does it identify exactly what some of those accounts are?
Expression of Ideas · Item 4

Research notes

Sensor B detects movement up to 27 meters.
Sensor A uses less power than Sensor B.
The student wants to compare detection range.

Expression of Ideas · Item 5

Coating B dried in 6 hours.

E5Level 3

Expression of Ideas · Item 6

Synthesis: General Claim Plus Example

Research notes
Some desert plants conserve water through specialized structures.
The saguaro cactus has pleated sides.
The pleats expand after rainfall.
The student wants to introduce the saguaro as an example supporting the general claim.
E6Level 4
Clever path: underline the action word in the goal. “Introduce as an example” requires the broad idea and the specific case in one sentence.
