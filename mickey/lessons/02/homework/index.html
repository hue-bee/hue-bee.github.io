Part A — Angle Relationships & Triangles

5 Questions · ~12 min

Digital SAT habit: before calculating, write the relationship that makes your equation valid. Diagrams are not necessarily drawn to scale.

Name it firstvertical → equallinear pair → 180°parallel lines → angle pairstriangle sum → 180°
Question 1 L2MC

Lines m and n are parallel. The marked angles are corresponding. What is the value of x?

Question 2 L2SPR

Two lines intersect. One angle measures (4y + 18)°, and an adjacent angle measures (7y − 3)°. What is the value of y?

Question 3 L3MC

An isosceles triangle has two congruent base angles, each measuring (3n + 4)°. The exterior angle at the vertex measures 128°. What is the value of n?

Question 4 L3MC

In triangle ABC, angle A measures 46°. The exterior angle at C measures 119°. What is the measure of angle B?

Efficiency check: Can you solve without first finding the interior angle at C?

Question 5 L4SPR

In the figure, lines p and q are parallel. A triangle is formed between them. The two remote interior angles shown measure (a + 12)° and (2a − 6)°. The exterior angle at the third vertex measures 96°. What is the value of a?

Part B — Similarity, Scale & Proportion

5 Questions · ~14 min

Prove or use similarity before setting a proportion. Keep corresponding vertices in the same order and label the scale factor.

Scale dialslength × kperimeter × karea × k²volume × k³
Question 6 L2MC

Triangles GHI and JKL are similar, where G ↔ J, H ↔ K, and I ↔ L. If GH = 15, JK = 25, and HI = 21, what is KL?

Question 7 L3MC

In triangle ABC, D lies on AB, E lies on AC, and DE is parallel to BC. If AD = 8, DB = 4, AE = 10, and EC = x, what is x?

Question 8 L3SPR

Two similar polygons have corresponding side lengths in the ratio 5:8. The perimeter of the larger polygon is 104. What is the perimeter of the smaller polygon?

Question 9 L4MC

Two similar triangles have corresponding side lengths in the ratio 4:7. If the area of the smaller triangle is 48 square units, what is the area of the larger triangle?

Question 10 L5SPR

A small right rectangular prism is enlarged so that every linear dimension is multiplied by 3/2. The small prism has volume 64 cubic units. What is the volume, in cubic units, of the enlarged prism?

Do not invent dimensions. Use the exponent that matches the measurement being scaled.
Match corresponding quantities; then choose k, k², or k³.Page 2 of 4

Part C — Right Triangles & Trigonometry

6 Questions · ~18 min

Mark the reference angle. Label opposite, adjacent, and hypotenuse before choosing sine, cosine, or tangent. Preserve exact values unless a decimal is requested.

Tool choice3 sides → Pythagoreanspecial angles → exact ratiosangle + 2 sides → trig
Question 11 L2MC

A right triangle has legs of length 9 and 40. What is the length of its hypotenuse?

Question 12 L2MC

In a 45°–45°–90° triangle, each leg has length 6√2. What is the length of the hypotenuse?

Question 13 L3MC

The longer leg of a 30°–60°–90° triangle is 11√3. What is the perimeter of the triangle?

Question 14 L3MC

In right triangle RST, angle T is 90°, RS = 29, RT = 21, and ST = 20. Which expression equals cos R?

Question 15 L4SPR

In a right triangle, tan θ = 5/12 and the hypotenuse has length 39. What is the length of the side opposite θ?

Question 16 L4MC

Angles u and v are the acute angles of a right triangle. If cos u = 8/17, which expression must equal 8/17?

Part D — Mixed Digital SAT Sprint

4 Questions · 8 min

Set an 8-minute timer. If a question exceeds 2 minutes, mark it, move on, and diagnose the delay afterward.

Question 17 L3MC

Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. One same-side interior angle measures (6z + 9)°, and the other measures (3z + 36)°. What is the measure of the larger angle?

Question 18 L4MC

A triangle is dilated by a scale factor of 1.5. The area of the image is 90 square units. What was the area of the original triangle?

Question 19 L4MC

From a point on level ground, the angle of elevation to the top of a flagpole is 34°. The point is 72 feet from the base. Which expression gives the flagpole's height h, in feet?

Question 20 L5MC

A 13-foot ladder leans against a vertical wall. Its base is moved 2 feet farther from the wall, from 5 feet away to 7 feet away, while the ladder length stays fixed. By how many feet does the top of the ladder move down the wall?
