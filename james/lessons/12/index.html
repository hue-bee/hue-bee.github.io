Deliver

Setup Blueprint

Make the Relationships Visible Before Algebra

Read this situation
A theater sold 680 tickets. It sold 140 more adult tickets than student tickets. The question asks for the number of student tickets.
Let s = student tickets
adult tickets = s + 140
student + adult = 680
target: student tickets
Why not start with 680 − 140?
Because the total contains two unknown groups. The difference describes the gap, not either group itself.

Guided Challenge 1 · Step-by-Step

Solve One Variable — Deliver a Different Quantity

9Level 4
At a fundraiser, 312 tickets were sold. There were 48 more adult tickets than child tickets. Adult tickets cost $14 and child tickets cost $9. How much more revenue came from adult tickets than child tickets?
Before algebra
What is the unknown base group? What is the final noun? Are they the same?
1
Define
Let c = number of child tickets. Then adult tickets = c + 48.
2
Relate
c + (c + 48) = 312.
3
Solve and label
The equation gives 132 child tickets and 180 adult tickets.
4
Deliver the requested difference
Evaluate 14(180) − 9(132). Enter only the final dollar amount.
Stop point
Finish the arithmetic yourself. The ticket counts are intermediate labels, not the requested response.

Guided Challenge 2 · Step-by-Step

Net Rate Solves the Time — Not the Asked Quantity

16Level 4
A tank starts with 840 gallons. Water drains at 32 gallons per minute while a pump adds 11 gallons per minute. When 399 gallons remain, how many gallons have drained out?
1
Define
Let t = minutes elapsed.
2
Model the net change
840 − (32 − 11)t = 399.
3
Solve and label
The equation gives t = 21 minutes.
4
Return to the actual verb
“Drained out” uses the 32-gallon rate, so evaluate 32(21).
Why 21 is not the final response
Twenty-one measures time. The prompt requests gallons drained, so the solved time must be converted through the drain rate.
Guided Challenge 3 · Step-by-Step

Undo the Chain — Then Read the Target Again

17Level 4
A store marks an item up 20%, discounts the marked price 10%, and adds 8.8% sales tax. The final payment is $117.504. What was the dollar amount of the discount?
Direction check
Starting from the final payment, which operation must be undone first? Why must the order reverse?
1
Define
Let p = original price.
2
Write the multiplier chain
p(1.20)(0.90)(1.088) = 117.504.
3
Solve and label stages
Original = $100; marked price = $120; price after discount = $108.
4
Deliver the discount amount
Compute marked price − discounted price. Enter the dollar amount only.
Percent versus dollar amount
The discount rate is already 10%. The question asks for money, so the final response must compare the two labeled prices.
