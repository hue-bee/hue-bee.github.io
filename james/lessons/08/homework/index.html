Platform at Dawn
Original instructional poem created for this activity
1Before the first train lifts its doors,
2the station holds its breath;
3a broom draws circles through the dust,
4the clocks rehearse their steps.
5I wait beneath the timetable
6that flickers, loses names;
7across the tracks, one window warms
8and steadies into flame.
9You said that leaving changes maps,
10not roads. I doubted then.
11Now rails divide the paling dark
12and join it end to end.
