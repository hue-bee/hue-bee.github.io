Use on Every Problem

The right answer is not the goal — the decision behind it is. Set up an equation before you calculate, and reread the last line before you circle anything.

The routine1. Underline the given percent2. Write new = old × (1 ± r)3. Reread: value or difference?

Part B: Reverse Percent

4 Problems

You are given the NEW amount and asked for the OLD one. Set up the equation before you divide.

Templatenew = old × (1 ± r)old = new ÷ (1 ± r)Increase → 1 + r  •  Decrease → 1 − r

After a 25% increase, a number becomes 80. What was the original number?

Answer
Problem 2 L2

Answer
Problem 3 L3

Answer
Problem 4 L4

A price of $88 already includes a 12% markup over the wholesale cost. Find the wholesale cost, then find the price after a separate 8% discount off that wholesale cost.

Answer
SHSAT Trap — flipping the factor: A 25% increase uses ×1.25, not ×0.75. Confusing the sign of r is the single most common reverse-percent error.

Answer

Part E: Function of a Detail

ELA · 3 Problems

A function question asks what job a detail does for the passage — not what it means. Cover the choices, say the job out loud, then look.

MethodAsk "why is this here?"Name the job, not the summaryEliminate "true but useless" choices
Passage — The Lighthouse Keeper
Marta had tended the lighthouse for thirty-one years, and in all that time she had never once let the lamp go dark. Ships passing in the fog knew her light the way children know a parent's voice. When the automated beacon was installed last spring, engineers offered to relieve her of the nightly climb. Marta thanked them, then climbed the stairs anyway, just to be certain.
Problem 9 L2

What is the function of the detail that Marta climbed the stairs "just to be certain," even after the automated beacon was installed?

Problem 10 L3

Why does the author compare Marta's light to "a parent's voice"?

SHSAT Trap — True But Useless: An answer choice can be a fact stated in the passage and still be wrong, because it doesn't name the job the detail is doing.
Problem 11 L4

In one sentence, explain the function of the phrase "thirty-one years" in the opening sentence. Then explain why a choice like "it tells us Marta is old" would be true but useless.
