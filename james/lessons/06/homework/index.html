Part A: Conversion Direction

6 Problems · 12 min

Write the unit fraction first. Check that the unwanted unit cancels — that tells you multiply or divide.

Rule Write units as a fraction — if it doesn't cancel, flip it
Problem 1 L1

1 meter = 100 centimeters. A ribbon is 350 centimeters long. How many meters is that?

Answer
Problem 2 L1

1 gallon = 4 quarts. A container holds 18 quarts. How many gallons is that?

Answer
Problem 3 L2

1 pound = 16 ounces. A recipe needs 2.5 pounds of flour. How many ounces should you measure?

Answer
Problem 4 L2

On planet Kelvo, 1 zorp = 0.6 tabs. You have 9 zorps and need the answer in tabs. Which operation gets you there?

Answer
Problem 5 L3

1 hour = 60 minutes, and 1 minute = 60 seconds. A show is 7,200 seconds long. How many hours is that? Chain two unit fractions.

Answer
Problem 6 L4

On planet Kelvo, 1 nix = 1.4 dabs, and 1 dab = 0.25 vims. You have 20 nixes and need the answer in vims. Name the Wrong-Direction trap answer choice before solving.

Answer

Part B: Unit Rates & Fractions

6 Problems · 15 min

Ask: is this number the FULL amount, or a piece of it? Rebuild the whole before you divide by hours or units.

Method 1. Is this the whole or a piece? 2. Add back what was removed 3. THEN divide
Problem 7 L1

Compute 45 ÷ (3/4). Before calculating — will the answer be bigger or smaller than 45? Now solve.

Answer
Problem 8 L2

A tutor earns $280 after a $40 platform fee is subtracted, across an 8-hour week. What was her hourly rate BEFORE the fee? Rebuild the whole first.

Answer
Problem 9 L3

Compute 72 ÷ (6/5). Two things happen at once here — estimate first: is the answer bigger or smaller than 72, and by about what factor? Then compute exactly.

Answer
Problem 10 L3

Two snack bags are for sale: a small bag has 3 cups for $4.50, and a large bag has 7 cups for $9.80. Which bag is the better unit rate per cup, and by how much per cup?

Answer
Problem 11 L4

A freelancer's paycheck shows $540 after a 10% platform fee was already subtracted, for 30 hours of work. Find the hourly rate BEFORE the fee. Careful — this is a reverse-percent, not a simple add-back.

Answer
Problem 12 L4

A worker's take-home pay is $399 after $56 is deducted for taxes and a $21 uniform fee, across a 25-hour week. What is the hourly rate BEFORE any deductions?

Answer

Part C: Similar Figures

6 Problems · 15 min

State which quantity you're scaling BEFORE you pick the exponent. Perimeter scales once, area scales twice.

Rule Perimeter ratio = k  ·  Area ratio = k²
Problem 13 L1

Two similar squares have a side ratio of 1:5. If the small square's area is 4, what is the big square's area? Which power of 5 do you need?

Answer
Problem 14 L2

Similar rectangles C and D have perimeters 20 and 60. What is the side ratio, and if rectangle C's area is 15, what is rectangle D's area?

Answer
Problem 15 L3

Two similar hexagons have areas 5 and 80. What is the ratio of their perimeters? Work backwards — find k from the area ratio first.

Answer
Problem 16 L3

A small model boat has a sail with area 2 square feet; the real boat's sail area is 128 times bigger. What linear scale factor was used, and how many times longer is the real mast than the model's mast?

Answer
Problem 17 L4

Two similar triangles have a side ratio of 2:7. The small triangle's perimeter is 18 and its area is 24. Find BOTH the big triangle's perimeter AND its area. Label which rule you used for each.

Answer
Problem 18 L4

A garden design is scaled up so its area increases by a factor of 225. A trap answer choice multiplies the fence length (perimeter) by 225 too. Explain why that's wrong, then find the correct perimeter scale factor.

Answer
