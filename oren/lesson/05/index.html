ELA · Detail Evidence

Measure the Word “Bustling”

Q11Level 2
Which sentence most directly supports the description of Lowell as a bustling industrial city?
Define before comparing
If “bustling” means active and crowded with work, what evidence would show the greatest scale of industry and people?
Related is not direct
Canals and large buildings show industrial infrastructure. Do they prove activity as directly as quantities of mills and workers?
Evidence candidates — paraphrased
Founding choice
Merchants selected the river site for water power.
Infrastructure
Canals powered large brick mill buildings.
Named landmarks
Several well-known mill buildings are listed.
Scale
Dozens of mills employed more than ten thousand workers.

A Strong Summary Keeps the Turning Point

Q14Level 4
Which sentence best summarizes the mill girls' experience as Lowell's dominant workforce?
Coverage test

Outcome
Departure
A summary that skips the turning point breaks the chain.
Step 1 · Map the arc
Beginning, turning point, response, outcome.
Step 2 · Compress
Keep causes and outcomes; remove decorative details.
Step 3 · Reject gaps
If a choice jumps from work to departure, ask what it omitted.
ELA · Direct Cause

Separate the Symptom from the Cause

Q16Level 3

Run the “because” test
“Lowell lost leadership because ____.” Which detail completes that sentence with the most direct competitive explanation?
A serious problem can still be the wrong answer
Low wages and poor conditions mattered to workers. The question, however, asks about industrial leadership.
