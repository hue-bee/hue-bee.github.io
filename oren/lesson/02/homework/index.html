⚠️ SHSAT Trap: Mixed Numbers into Multiplication

A common mistake is multiplying the whole number and fraction parts separately. Always convert a mixed number to an improper fraction before you multiply or divide.

Answer: cm

⚠️ SHSAT Trap: Miscounted Decimal Places

The single most common decimal error is placing the point in the wrong spot after multiplying. Count the digits after the point in each factor, then add those counts together — every time.

Part E — ELA: Modifiers

2 Questions · ~6 min

A modifier must sit right next to the noun it logically describes. A dangling modifier has nothing logical to describe; a misplaced modifier sits next to the wrong noun. Fix: move the phrase next to the correct noun.

Q1. Which sentence fixes the dangling modifier? "Covered in frosting, Maria cut the cake." L2
Q2. Identify the misplaced modifier: "She almost finished all of her homework before dinner." L3
