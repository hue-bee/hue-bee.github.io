CAT Frame · First-Pass Accuracy

Commit Only After You Can Name the Job

Think before the choices
If a detail is vivid, surprising, or true, does that automatically make it relevant to the question? What extra sentence must you be able to say: “The author includes this detail in order to ___”?
The CAT-safe scratch line
WHOLE → DETAIL → JOB → CHOICE
Write a two- to four-word prediction before opening the answer choices. It reduces “true-but-wrong” attraction.
Adaptive-test trap · mood matching
A choice can match the passage’s mood yet misstate the cited sentence’s function. On a no-return item, verify the exact link before advancing.

Zone 1 · Distractor Anatomy

True of the Passage ≠ The Detail’s Job

The topic magnet
It repeats a major idea from the text, so it feels familiar. But the cited detail may not develop that idea at this exact moment.
True globally + unsupported locally = reject
The bridge test
Point to a word, image, contrast, sequence, or shift inside the cited detail. Then explain how that feature changes what the reader understands.
Detail evidence → function verb → purpose
Three elimination questions
1. Does the choice describe the correct moment? 2. Does it claim a change the character or speaker never makes? 3. Could I delete the cited detail and still make the same claim? If yes, the answer may be merely topical.
Method only · no answer key in this deck

Forecast trap

Zone 5 · Core Statistics

Four Questions, Four Different Statistics

MEAN · fair share
sum ÷ count
Sensitive to every value, especially extremes.
MEDIAN · middle rank
order first
Depends on position; for even count, average the two middle values.
MODE · most frequent
highest frequency
A set may have none, one, or several modes.
RANGE · spread endpoints
max − min
Uses only two values; says nothing about the interior shape.

Zone 6 · Averages

Stop Averaging Averages: Recover the Total

SUM = MEAN × COUNT
The mean is a compressed description. Multiplying by the count decompresses it back into a total.
Why this is clever
A missing-value problem hides one contribution inside a known total. A new-average problem compares two totals. A weighted-average problem joins two totals. The same move solves all three.

Zone 6 · Weighted Averages

Different Group Sizes Carry Different Weight

combined mean = (n₁m₁ + n₂m₂) ÷ (n₁ + n₂)
Why not average the two means?
Would a class of 30 students and a tutoring group of 2 students deserve equal influence? What quantity tells you how much “mass” each mean represents?
Mental estimate first
The combined mean must lie between the group means and closer to the mean of the larger group.
Zone 6 · Weighted Practice

Frequency Tables & Groups Easy to Hard

18Level 1
Values 4 and 10 occur 3 times and 1 time, respectively. Find the mean.
19Level 2
Group A: 6 students, mean 14. Group B: 4 students, mean 19. Find the combined mean.
20Level 3
A 20-student class has mean 78. After 5 students join, the 25-student mean is 80. Find the new students’ mean.
21Level 4
Two groups have means 72 and 84. Their combined mean is 81. Without knowing either count, determine the ratio of the group sizes.
Advanced shortcut · balance distances
If the combined mean is much closer to one group’s mean, that group must be larger. The size ratio is inverse to the distances from the combined mean.
Zone 6 · Robustness

When a Value Moves, Which Statistic Notices?

Mean question
The total increases while the count stays fixed. What direction must the mean move? Can you predict the exact change from the moved distance?
Median question
The middle rank is unchanged. Does moving an endpoint alter which value occupies the middle position?
Do not memorize “median never changes”
Adding or removing a value can shift the middle position. Reorder and recount; robustness is not immunity.

Session Wrap-Up

What We Locked In Today

Purpose Funnel
Whole-text purpose → local detail → function verb → answer choice. A topical answer without a bridge is not enough.
Scope & Clock
Match a choice to the cited moment. Reject conclusions that belong earlier, later, or nowhere in the evidence window.
Data Display Scan
Read title, unit, scale, and task before computing. Visual misreads are model errors, not small arithmetic slips.
Average Engine
Mean × count restores the total. Totals unlock missing values, changed means, frequency tables, and weighted groups.
Final verbal checkpoint
Can you explain why a choice is wrong without first knowing which choice is right? Can you predict a new mean’s direction before calculating it?
