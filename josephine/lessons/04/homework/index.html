A Quieter School Cafeteria

[1] At Midtown Community School, lunchtime conversations often dissolved into a blur of echoes. [2] The cafeteria’s concrete walls were durable ___ they reflected nearly every sound back into the room. [3] Because replacing the walls would have been costly, the student design club searched for a smaller intervention.

[4] Club members tested panels made from recycled denim, cork, and compressed paper. [5] Each material was hung in the same corner for one week, and a sound meter recorded the results. [6] For example, the students compared how well the panels reduced echoes before choosing a material.

[7] After three trials, the cork panels performed best and was also the easiest to clean. [8] Covered with student-designed patterns, the facilities director approved the panels for the entire cafeteria. [9] Since the installation, teachers report that students can speak comfortably without raising their voices.
