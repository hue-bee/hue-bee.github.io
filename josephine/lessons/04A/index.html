0:17–0:20 · Repaired Domain 1 of 4

Percent Change: Protect the Base

M5Level 3

A school store lowers a sweatshirt’s price from \(\$64\) to \(\$56\). What is the percent decrease?

Enter the numerical percent only, without the percent sign. Explain which price represents \(100\%\).

Guided path · Three steps, then finish

Name the base

Label the original price “\(100\%\).”

Build the fraction

\((\text{Original} - \text{new}) \div \text{original}\). Substitute before calculating.

Convert and inspect

Multiply by \(100\). Finish the arithmetic; check the requested entry form.

The original is your ruler

Which amount represents \(100\%\)? Would changing that ruler change the question?

0:20–0:23 · Repaired Domain 2 of 4

Averages: Recover the Hidden Total

M6Level 3

Four practice rounds have a mean score of \(18\) points. A fifth round is added, and the mean of all five rounds is \(20\) points. How many points were scored in the fifth round?

Guided path · Three steps, then finish

Recover the old total

\(\text{Old mean} \times \text{old count}\). Write that product.

Recover the new total

\(\text{New mean} \times \text{new count}\), including the added round.

Find the addition

\(\text{New total} - \text{old total}\). Finish; check the five-round mean.

Recover the receipt

A mean summarizes a total. Which multiplication recovers that total before and after the new round?

0:23–0:26 · Repaired Domain 3 of 4

Multi-Select: Judge Every Option

M7Level 4

Select all values of \(x\) that satisfy the inequality.

\(-6 < -2x + 2 \leq 8\)

Two gates: Passing only one condition is not enough. Which endpoint symbol would allow equality?

Guided path · Three steps, then select

Split the conditions

Write \(-6 < -2x + 2\) and \(-2x + 2 \leq 8\).

Undo carefully

Subtract \(2\). When dividing by a negative, reverse the inequality.

Check every option

Substitute in the original. Select only values that satisfy both conditions.
