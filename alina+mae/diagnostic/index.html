SHSAT CAT Diagnostic

Two 40-minute adaptive sections in one test: original ELA and Math content, preserved CAT-style controls, and one combined report after both sections are complete.

Recommended administration: Choose either section first. You will automatically continue to the other section’s start screen when the first ends, with a short break if desired. Each section keeps its own 40-minute clock. Both reports stay hidden until the entire test is finished; then print both together or save one PDF. The official test instead uses one 180-minute session for both 50-question sections.
